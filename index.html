Device Update Monitoring

We keep a private log of the devices on your network and notify you when firmware or security updates are available. You stay in control — we just make sure nothing important gets missed.

How It Works

  • We record your devices during the initial setup or diagnostic (name, model, current version).
  • When a relevant update is released, you receive a simple notification.
  • You can update the device yourself and reply to confirm, or request a quick remote check.
  • Prefer hands-off? Schedule a short on-site visit for a small fee.

Included with Ongoing Support

Common Questions

No. We start with a diagnostic. Many homes only need better placement, an additional access point, or light segmentation.

Yes. We work past the demarcation point, so your existing internet service stays in place.

That’s fine. You’ll receive a clear written report you can use yourself or with another installer.

No. Smaller homes with dead zones, many devices, or automation plans benefit just as much.

We log your devices and notify you of important updates. You can update them yourself and confirm, or we can handle it on-site for a small fee. It’s included with the monthly support plan.
