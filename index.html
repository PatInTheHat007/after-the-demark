The demarc

Where your provider's help ends

The "demarc" (demarcation point) is the box where your internet provider's responsibility stops, usually your modem. Coverage, speed in each room, and security after that box are left to you.

Renting from your provider

"Managed Wi-Fi" or mesh pods as a monthly add-on. Convenient, but you never own it, you get little control, and the bill never ends.

Owning your network

Equipment that's yours. Strong Wi-Fi in every room, every device visible, smart devices kept separate, and no rental fees.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is the "demarc"?

The demarcation point, or "demarc," is where your internet provider's responsibility ends, usually at the modem or the box where the line enters your home. Everything after that (your router, Wi-Fi, wiring, and devices) is up to you. That's where we come in, hence the name.

What area do you serve?

Elmira, Corning, Horseheads, and the surrounding Southern Tier, plus the Finger Lakes: Watkins Glen, Seneca Lake, Hammondsport, Keuka Lake, Penn Yan, and nearby. If you're a little outside that, call or text and ask.

Can't my internet provider fix my Wi-Fi?

Your provider makes sure internet reaches the modem. They usually won't fix coverage, wiring, or security inside your home, and their answer is often to rent you another box. That's why so many people pay for fast internet and still have slow Wi-Fi in the bedroom.

Will this work with my current internet provider?

Yes. We work after the demarc, so your existing internet service stays exactly as it is.

Do I have to replace all my equipment?

No. Many homes only need better placement, one extra access point, or a separate network for cameras and smart devices. The assessment tells you exactly what's worth doing.

Do I have to hire you after the assessment?

No. The report is yours to keep, with DIY fixes and priced options. If you do hire us within 60 days, the full assessment fee is credited toward the work.

What's the difference between the Checkup and the Assessment?

The $99 Wi-Fi Checkup is a quick 45-minute visit focused on your problem rooms, with findings explained on the spot. The full Assessment covers every room, your security settings, every device, and your wiring, and comes with a written report and priced options. If you start with a Checkup, the $99 is credited toward the full Assessment.

Omada or UniFi: which should I choose?

Both are excellent, business-grade systems. Omada (Essentials) gives you the same core features for less. UniFi (Pro) has a more polished app and lets you add cameras and door access on the same system. We'll recommend one based on your home and budget.

Do you work on Airbnbs and vacation rentals?

Yes, and especially in the Finger Lakes. We cover outbuildings and docks, set up a guest network that's separate from your own devices, add keyless entry and leak or freeze alerts, and can monitor the property remotely during the season.

Do you work with renters?

Yes. Many improvements, like better placement or adding an access point, don't need permanent changes. If something would, we'll talk about it first.

Will you put holes in my walls?

As few and as small as possible. Most cable runs drop down from the attic. Any access holes we make are patched and left ready to paint.

Do you keep access to my network?

Only if you join a Care Plan, and you can remove it anytime. At the end of every job you get every admin password. It's your network.

How do payments work?

Assessments are paid at booking. Small repairs are paid on completion. Whole-home projects are 50% at signing (which covers equipment), 40% after the cabling is done, and 10% at the final walkthrough.
