The Demarcation Point Is Where Help Ends

Common Questions

What area do you serve?

Elmira, Corning, Horseheads, and the surrounding Chemung and Steuben County area. If you’re a little outside that, call or text and ask.

Do I have to replace all my equipment?

No. Many homes only need better placement, one extra access point, or a separate network for cameras and smart devices.

Will this work with my current ISP?

Yes. We work past the demarcation point. Your existing internet service stays.

How long does the diagnostic take?

It’s a single on-site visit. Time depends on the size of your home and how many devices you have; you’ll get an estimate when you book.

What if I only want the diagnostic?

That’s completely fine. You’ll get a clear written report either way.

Do you work with renters?

Yes. Many improvements, like better placement or adding an access point, don’t need permanent changes. If something would, we’ll talk about it first.

Do I need the monthly plan?

No. It’s optional. Everything we install is yours, and it works without any subscription.

How does device update monitoring work?

With the Network Health Plan we log your devices and notify you of important updates. You can update them yourself or we can handle it on-site.
